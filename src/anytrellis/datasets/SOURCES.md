# Sources of the bundled datasets

The datasets in this folder (`anytrellis.datasets.load`) are taken from
[vega-datasets](https://github.com/vega/vega-datasets) 2.8.0, with snake_case column names
and readable categories. They are not covered by anytrellis's MIT licence: each keeps the
terms of its original source, as vega-datasets documents them (its code is BSD-3-Clause).

| dataset | source | licence |
|---|---|---|
| anscombe | F. J. Anscombe (1973), *Graphs in Statistical Analysis*, The American Statistician 27(1):17-21 | not specified (published data) |
| barley | Immer, Hayes and Powers (1934); Wiebe et al. (1940), *Yields of Barley Varieties in the United States and Canada, 1932-36*, USDA; Cleveland (1993), *Visualizing Data* | not specified (Minnesota Agricultural Experiment Station) |
| cars | StatLib Datasets Archive, <http://lib.stat.cmu.edu/datasets/> | not specified; distributed in 1982 for educational and scientific purposes |
| gapminder | Gapminder Foundation, <https://www.gapminder.org/data/> | CC BY 4.0: free material from www.gapminder.org |
| penguins | Palmer Station Antarctica LTER; Horst, Hill and Gorman, palmerpenguins, <https://github.com/allisonhorst/palmerpenguins> | CC0 1.0 |
| population | IPUMS USA, <https://usa.ipums.org/usa/> | IPUMS terms of use, <https://www.ipums.org/about/terms> |
| seattle_weather | NOAA National Climatic Data Center | U.S. Government work (public domain) |
| stocks | as distributed with vega-datasets | not specified |

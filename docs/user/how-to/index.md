# How-to guides

Steps for a task you already have in mind. Each guide starts from a plot like the one in
[Your first trellis plot](../tutorials/first-plot.md).

**Getting started**

- [Install anytrellis](install.md)
- [Plot your own tables](tables.md): Polars, pandas, PyArrow, DuckDB or a dict of columns.

**Panels and scales**

- [Choose a facet layout](layout.md): a wrap or a grid, columns, height, titles.
- [Share or free the scales](scales.md): per row, per column, per panel.
- [Use a log scale](log.md)

**Layers**

- [Draw lines, points and bars](marks.md)
- [Draw areas, bands, rects and text](more-marks.md): ranges, heatmaps, labels, reference
  lines.
- [Show distributions](distributions.md): histograms, box plots, violins, densities and error bars.
- [Fit a smooth line](smooth.md): loess or least squares, with a confidence band.
- [Colours, legends and order](colours.md)

**Working with plots**

- [Link plots](link.md): one selection across separate plots.
- [Plot large data](large.md): millions of marks, hundreds of panels.
- [Page through many panels](pages.md): sort and filter them by each panel's statistics.
- [Share a plot](share.md): a page that opens without Python.
- [Use anytrellis in marimo](marimo.md)

**Coming from another library**

- [ggplot2 or seaborn](from-ggplot2-seaborn.md)
- [Altair or lattice](from-altair-lattice.md)

**When something goes wrong**

- [Fix a plot that doesn't show](troubleshooting.md)
